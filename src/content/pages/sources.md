# Sources

The answers on this site rest on peer-reviewed research.
Each question lists the papers that support it under **References**.
This page lists the journals where that research is published.

## Journals

- [Applied Animal Behaviour Science](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/applied-animal-behaviour-science) — Elsevier, for the International Society for Applied Ethology.
  Behaviour and welfare of domestic animals, including much dog behaviour and training research.
- [Animal Behaviour](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/animal-behaviour) — Elsevier, for the Association for the Study of Animal Behaviour.
  Fundamental behaviour research across species, including dogs and wolves.
- [Animal Cognition](https://link.springer.com/journal/10071) — Springer, open access.
  Comparative cognition, with a large body of dog cognition studies.
- [Animal Welfare](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/animal-welfare) — Cambridge University Press, for the Universities Federation for Animal Welfare; open access.
  Welfare science, including companion and kennelled dogs.
- [Anthrozoös](https://www.tandfonline.com/toc/rfan20/current) — Taylor & Francis, for the International Society for Anthrozoology.
  Human–animal interaction, including the dog–owner relationship and dog walking.
- [Companion Animal Health and Genetics](https://link.springer.com/journal/40575) — BioMed Central, open access.
  Health, disease and genetics of companion animals; formerly Canine Medicine and Genetics.
- [Frontiers in Veterinary Science](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/veterinary-science) — Frontiers, open access.
  Broad veterinary science, with an animal behaviour and welfare section that publishes many dog studies.
- [JAVMA](https://avmajournals.avma.org/view/journals/javma/javma-overview.xml) — American Veterinary Medical Association.
  Clinical veterinary medicine and practice.
- [Journal of Comparative Psychology](https://www.apa.org/pubs/journals/com) — American Psychological Association.
  Comparative cognition and social behaviour, including dog cognition.
- [Journal of Small Animal Practice](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/17485827) — Wiley, for the British Small Animal Veterinary Association.
  Clinical practice for dogs and cats, including toxicology case series.
- [Journal of Veterinary Behavior](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-veterinary-behavior) — Elsevier.
  Clinical behaviour, behaviour problems and training methods in dogs and cats.
- [Journal of Veterinary Internal Medicine](https://academic.oup.com/jvim) — American College of Veterinary Internal Medicine; open access.
  Internal medicine in dogs and cats.
- [Veterinary Record](https://bvajournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/20427670) — Wiley, for the British Veterinary Association.
  Companion-animal epidemiology, poisoning and welfare.
