# How often should I walk my dog?

I could not find a study that sets a daily amount of exercise that suits every dog.
Common rules, such as two walks a day, are a starting point rather than a finding; the studies I found that tested them were funded by pet-food companies, which I don't cite.

## What the research shows

- **Activity falls with age.** In the Dog Aging Project, owners of [more than 23,000 dogs](https://doi.org/10.1093/gerona/glac099) reported that older dogs were less active than younger ones.
- **Longer walks go with a healthier weight.** In a Dutch survey of [2,303 dog owners](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0237429), about 20% of dogs walked for under 30 minutes a day were overweight, against 5% of dogs walked for 90 minutes or more.
  Owners judged their own dog's body condition, few dogs were in the shortest group, and a survey cannot show that walking caused the difference.
- **Activity and the ageing brain.** In older dogs, being less active is associated with signs of cognitive decline (see [Does exercise help keep an older dog's mind sharp?](does-exercise-help-an-older-dogs-mind.md)).

## What changes the answer

- **Size and setting:** in the Dog Aging Project, [larger dogs were more active](https://doi.org/10.1093/gerona/glac099) than smaller dogs, especially when young, and rural dogs were more active than suburban or urban dogs.
  All of this was reported by owners, who were self-selected.
- **Age:** for puppies of four large breeds, off-lead exercise on soft ground in the first three months [lowered the risk of hip dysplasia](https://doi.org/10.2460/ajvr.73.6.838), while climbing stairs raised it.
- **Heat:** exercise, not hot cars, caused [74% of heatstroke cases](https://doi.org/10.3390/ani10081324) in UK dogs, and flat-faced breeds were at greater risk.
  French Bulldogs and Pugs with more severe breathing problems were [less active](https://doi.org/10.1017/awf.2023.80).

## Signs your dog may need more exercise

- Weight gain
- Anxiety: dogs with noise sensitivity or separation anxiety [got less daily exercise](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0141907), though this does not show that exercise prevents anxiety

Destructive chewing and evening restlessness are often blamed on too little exercise, but this has not been studied.

Your vet can suggest a level that suits your dog.
