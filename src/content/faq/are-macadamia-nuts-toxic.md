# Are macadamia nuts toxic to dogs?

Macadamia nuts are toxic to dogs.
How they cause harm is not known.

No deaths have been reported, but the signs are distressing.

## Signs of macadamia nut poisoning

Signs usually appear within 12 hours:

- Weakness, especially in the back legs
- Tremors and unsteadiness
- Raised body temperature

## How dangerous is it?

- **Most dogs recover fully** within one to two days, with little treatment.
- **Chocolate-covered macadamia nuts** add the dangers of [chocolate](is-chocolate-toxic.md).

### At what dose do symptoms appear?

Symptoms have appeared after as little as [0.7 g of nuts per kg](https://doi.org/10.3389/fvets.2016.00026) of body weight.

For example, a poodle of 3.8 kg could show symptoms after eating about 2.7 g, which is roughly a single nut.

## What to do?

Call your vet or an animal poison hotline straight away.
Note what was eaten, roughly how much, and when.

### What will the Vet do?

Vets may make your dog vomit if it ate the nuts recently and has no signs yet.
Otherwise they will observe your dog until it recovers.
