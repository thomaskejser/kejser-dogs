Many things that are safe for people are dangerous for dogs.
Each substance I have looked into is listed below.

This list is not complete.
If your dog has eaten something you are unsure about, call your vet or an animal poison helpline.

For obvious, ethical reasons, few experiments have been run to determine exact, toxic doses.
Where studies are available, I have quoted them.
