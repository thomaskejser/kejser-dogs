Alcohol is toxic to dogs.
It slows down the nervous system, and raw bread dough made with yeast produces alcohol inside the stomach.

High doses can result in coma and death.

## Signs of alcohol poisoning

Signs usually appear [within an hour](https://doi.org/10.3389/fvets.2016.00026):

- Vomiting
- Unsteadiness and drowsiness
- Low body temperature
- Collapse or coma in severe cases

## How dangerous is it?

- **Alcoholic drinks** are the obvious source.
- **Raw yeast dough** keeps fermenting in the warm stomach, producing alcohol and gas.
- **Rotting fruit** can also contain alcohol: a dog died after eating large amounts of rotten apples.

### At what dose do symptoms appear?

No study has found a safe or toxic dose for dogs, so treat any amount as dangerous.

## What to do?

Call your vet or an animal poison hotline straight away.
Note what was eaten or drunk, roughly how much, and when.

### What will the Vet do?

Vets will monitor your dog closely and give supportive care; most dogs recover this way.
Dialysis can remove alcohol from the blood: in [one dog](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1476-4431.2011.00652.x) that had not improved after 9 hours of supportive care, it led to a rapid recovery.
