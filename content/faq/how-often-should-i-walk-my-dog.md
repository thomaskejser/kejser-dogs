There is no research-based daily amount of exercise that fits every dog.
Even the common recommendation of at least 30 minutes a day [came mainly from expert opinion](https://doi.org/10.1017/jns.2017.7).
A rule of thumb is two walks a day, but treat that as a starting point rather than a finding: one walk a day was [no worse for a dog's weight](https://doi.org/10.1017/jns.2017.6) than two or more.

## What the research shows

- **Dogs vary enormously.** Activity trackers worn by [28,562 dogs](https://doi.org/10.3389/fvets.2025.1572794) recorded an average of 51 active minutes a day, ranging from under 10 minutes to over 600.
- **Longer walks go with a healthier weight.** Compared with dogs walked for over an hour a day, dogs walked for 10 minutes or less were more than twice as likely to be overweight, and dogs that took fewer [steps a day](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0007114511001814) carried more body fat.
- **Many dogs get less than breed guidelines suggest.** In a UK survey, 53% of dogs did not meet the Kennel Club's guidelines for their breed, including 92% of Labradors, though those guidelines are not themselves based on research.
- **Activity and the ageing brain.** In older dogs, being less active is associated with signs of cognitive decline (see [Does exercise help keep an older dog's mind sharp?](/faq/does-exercise-help-an-older-dogs-mind)).

## What changes the answer

- **Size:** larger breeds were more active in the tracker study, and owners walk bigger dogs for longer: small dogs were less likely to get more than 30 minutes a day (55%) than medium (72%) or large dogs (75%).
  A [smaller tracker study](https://doi.org/10.1017/jns.2014.49) found no difference by weight, so lifestyle may matter as much as breed.
- **Age:** dogs become less active and rest more as they age.
  For puppies of four large breeds, off-lead exercise on soft ground in the first three months [lowered the risk of hip dysplasia](https://doi.org/10.2460/ajvr.73.6.838), while climbing stairs raised it.
- **Heat:** exercise, not hot cars, caused [74% of heatstroke cases](https://doi.org/10.3390/ani10081324) in UK dogs, and flat-faced breeds were at greater risk.
  French Bulldogs and Pugs with more severe breathing problems were [less active](https://doi.org/10.1017/awf.2023.80).

## Signs your dog may need more exercise

- Weight gain
- Anxiety: dogs with noise sensitivity or separation anxiety [got less daily exercise](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0141907), though this does not show that exercise prevents anxiety

Destructive chewing and evening restlessness are often blamed on too little exercise, but this has not been studied.

Your vet can suggest a level that suits your dog.
